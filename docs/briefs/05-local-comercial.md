# Brief 05 — Local comercial y oficinas, Pocitos (Montevideo)

Benchmark role: **professional delivery**: the best documentation and workflow demonstration.

## Site

- Corner lot in Pocitos: 12 × 25 m, streets to the south and east.
- Latitude −34.9110, longitude −56.1500, about 15 m above sea level. CRS EPSG:5382.

## Programme (350–600 m², ground + 2 floors)

| Level | Content |
|---|---|
| Planta baja | Café (60 seats) with counter, kitchen and storage; corner storefront; accessible toilet; entrance to the offices |
| Nivel 1 | Open office (16 workstations), two meeting rooms, manager's office, kitchenette, toilets |
| Nivel 2 | Co-working, terrace, storage |
| Exterior | Loading bay on the side street, 2 parking spaces, bicycle racks |

## Construction

- Concrete frame, glazed storefront on both streets, aluminium curtain wall on the corner.
- Fit-out: suspended ceilings, partitions, finishes per room.

## The killer feature

Concept to a **complete drawing package quickly**, then change the storefront (a different mullion layout and a
second door) and the café layout, and show the documentation updating: plans, elevations, finish schedule, door
schedule and the delivery package.

## Deliverables

Geolocated IFC; furnished plans; reflected ceiling plans with lighting; elevations of both streets; interior
elevations of the café counter; finish, door and furniture schedules; sheets; delivery package.

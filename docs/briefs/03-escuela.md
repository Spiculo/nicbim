# Brief 03 — Escuela, Garzón (Maldonado)

Benchmark role: **architectural capability**: the most architecturally impressive of the five. Inspired by the
typology of schools in the landscape of Maldonado (mass timber, classrooms opening to the outside); it does not
reproduce any existing building.

## Site

- Rural-edge site near Pueblo Garzón: about 120 × 90 m, gently sloping (2 m) to the south-west.
- Latitude −34.6020, longitude −54.5490, about 80 m above sea level. CRS EPSG:5382 (UTM 21S) — Garzón is at
  the edge of zone 21; keep the project in 21S like the rest of Uruguay's official cartography.

## Programme (1,000–1,500 m², one storey with a partial upper level)

- 6 classrooms (55–60 m² each) in two wings, each opening to its own outdoor classroom.
- Library and resource room (90 m²) with a double-height reading space.
- Multipurpose hall (180 m², 6 m clear height) that doubles as gym and assembly.
- Administration (director, secretary, teachers' room), infirmary.
- Cafeteria and kitchen (120 m²).
- Toilets per wing, accessible toilets, cleaners' store.
- Covered gallery linking the wings; playground, vegetable garden and sports court.

## Construction

- Glulam frame (columns 20 × 20 cm, beams 20 × 60 cm) on a 6 × 6 m grid; CLT roof panels.
- Mono-pitch roofs per wing (different slopes) and a raised roof over the hall with clerestory light.
- Curtain walls to the outdoor classrooms; deep eaves (1.5 m) for summer shading.
- Accessible everywhere: ramps at 8 % maximum, no steps at the entrances.

## The killer feature

Turn the design into a **structured building**: rooms with names, numbers, areas, occupancy and finishes; a room
schedule and a door schedule that come straight from the model; sections through the hall and the library that
explain the timber structure.

## Deliverables

Geolocated IFC; site plan; plans 1:100; sections through hall and library; elevations; room and door schedules;
structural grid drawing; sheets and the delivery package.

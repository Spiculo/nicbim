# Brief 04 — Bodega, Canelones

Benchmark role: **BIM capability**: the most technically impressive. A small winery with a plausible process,
inspired by Uruguayan wineries; it does not reproduce any existing building.

## Site

- Vineyard plot near Progreso, Canelones: building area 70 × 40 m, flat; access road to the north.
- Latitude −34.6670, longitude −56.2190, about 45 m above sea level. CRS EPSG:5382.

## Programme (800–1,500 m²)

| Zone | Content |
|---|---|
| Reception | Grape reception, covered loading bay (2 truck bays), destemmer and press area |
| Fermentation hall | 12 stainless tanks (Ø 1.8 m, 3.2 m high) in two rows, catwalk at +3.00 |
| Barrel cellar | Half-buried, 300 barrels in racks of 2 levels, controlled climate |
| Bottling and storage | Bottling line, pallet racking, dispatch |
| Offices and visitors | Office, lab, tasting room with a view over the cellar, toilets |
| Services | Plant room (chillers), electrical room, water treatment |

## Construction

- Steel portal frames, 20 m span at 6 m centres; 8 m eaves in the fermentation hall.
- Concrete floors with falls to drainage channels; the cellar in concrete retaining walls.
- MEP: process water, drainage (with slopes), chilled-water pipes to the tanks, electrical circuits.

## The killer feature

An **information-rich, coordinated model**: structural frame analysed; tanks, racks, press and forklift as
equipment with data; pipes and drainage with slopes; a clash check between structure, equipment and pipes; model
filters and phases (existing vineyard shed, new building); area and volume quantities.

## Deliverables

Geolocated IFC; plans, sections and elevations; structure drawing; equipment schedule; clash report (BCF); sheets
and the delivery package.

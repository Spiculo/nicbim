# Brief 01 — Casa urbana, Parque Rodó (Montevideo)

Benchmark role: **ease of use**. The most polished of the five: beautiful, easy to read, the project a student or
a small studio recognises at once.

## Site

- Typical Montevideo urban lot between party walls: **8.66 m frontage × 30 m depth**, flat, street to the north.
- Location: Parque Rodó, near Br. España and Av. Julio Herrera y Reissig. Latitude −34.9145, longitude −56.1640,
  about 22 m above sea level. CRS **EPSG:5382** (SIRGAS-ROU98 / UTM 21S). The lot's front line runs 18° east of
  true east (`north` 18).
- Front setback 3 m (garden), lateral party walls on both sides, back patio at least 6 m deep.

## Programme (2 storeys, 180–240 m²)

| Level | Rooms |
|---|---|
| Planta baja (±0.00) | Entrance and stair hall, living room facing the patio, kitchen-dining (open), toilet, laundry, garage or bicycle store at the front, covered gallery to the patio |
| Planta alta (+3.00) | Main bedroom with bathroom and dressing, two bedrooms, shared bathroom, study/terrace at the front |
| Azotea (+6.00) | Accessible roof terrace with parapet (1.10 m), water tank, solar panels area |

## Construction

- Party walls: 30 cm brick masonry. Façades: 30 cm cavity wall, white render. Interior walls 15 cm.
- Concrete slabs 20 cm; flat roof with parapets.
- Straight or U stair, 1.0 m clear, risers ≤ 17.5 cm.
- Large openings to the patio (sliding glass, 2.4 m high), smaller vertical windows on the street façade.

## Deliverables

1. Geolocated IFC (IFC4), levels Planta baja / Planta alta / Azotea.
2. Plans of every level at 1:50 with rooms and areas, two sections (one through the stair), street and patio
   elevations.
3. Room/area schedule, door and window schedules.
4. Sheets A1: plans; sections and elevations; schedules and a 3D view.
5. Delivery package with the drawing set PDF.
6. **The design change:** widen the patio window from 4.0 m to 5.0 m and move the kitchen island; the plans,
   sections, schedules and the delivery package update.

## What it should show

Massing to walls, slabs, roof and openings; stairs; furniture from the library; rooms and area schedule;
elevations and sections that read well on paper; materials and a rendered 3D view.
